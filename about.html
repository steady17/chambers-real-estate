About Chambers

Source of Your Best Experience.

Source of Your Best Experience. Chambers Real Estate was founded in 2020 and is based in Kano, Nigeria.

Welcome

Bridging the gap in Kano's property market.

The Nigerian property market has been largely traditional, but with over 108 million internet users in the country, we saw an opportunity to bridge the gap. Especially in cities like Kano, the industry has been slow to adopt digital and social media marketing.

Our aim is to create a unified platform for the property market, making it accessible to all.

2020

Chambers Real Estate is founded in Kano, with a focus on property solutions, brokerage and investment for the local market.

Today

Chambers works with buyers, tenants and investors across Kano, and leads a growing set of residential development projects in the city.

Mission & Vision

Mission, vision and long-term vision

Mission Statement

Our mission is to pioneer the transformation of the Nigerian property market, transitioning it from a traditional model to a cutting-edge digital marketplace. We achieve this by harnessing the full potential of emerging technologies, with the aim of relieving individuals, agents, and companies of the challenges associated with property searches. Our ultimate goal is to deliver the highest levels of satisfaction to all stakeholders. Additionally, we provide a secure haven for investors and elevate your living standards and financial outlook with our world-class, top-notch designs.

Vision

Our vision is to become the foremost, most trusted, and reputable digital property marketplace in Africa.

Long Term Vision

In the long term, our ambition is to rank among the foremost real estate development and investment firms in Nigeria and across the entire African continent. We aim to achieve this by leading the way with innovative technological advancements in real estate, including a unified website connecting agents and property seekers, as well as a comprehensive range of property services.

Why choose us

Registered, certified, client-focused

We are registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission of Nigeria, and our agents are certified by the Property Agents and Landlords Association of Nigeria (PALDAN). At Chambers Real Estate, we are dedicated to assisting our clients and investors in achieving their property goals. We personalize their experiences, foster innovation, and strive to make a positive impact.

Where we operate

While we operate as a digitally based company, you can find our office at the heart of Kano State, specifically at No. 45 Lamido Crescent, Nasarawa GRA. Our services, although primarily accessible online, are available to all Nigerians across the country, provided they have internet access and a mobile or computer device. Currently, we focus our operations within the Kano metropolis.

Core values

Our core values guide our decision-making, promote teamwork, facilitate efficient collaboration among our employees, swiftly communicate our principles to clients and customers, and ensure the selection of employees with the right attitude.
